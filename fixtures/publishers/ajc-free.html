Braves Win Home Opener Behind Late Rally

By Sam Sports

The Braves scored three runs in the eighth inning on Thursday to come from behind and win their home opener before a sellout crowd.

The bullpen held the lead in the ninth, and fans stayed late to celebrate the first win of the new season at the ballpark.
