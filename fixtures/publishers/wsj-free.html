Markets Hold Steady as Investors Await Rate Decision

By Casey Markets

Stocks were little changed on Wednesday as investors waited for the central bank to signal its next move on interest rates.

Analysts said trading was thin ahead of the announcement, with many large funds staying on the sidelines for the session.
