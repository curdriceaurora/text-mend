Severe Storms Expected Across the Southeast This Weekend

By Pat Weather

Forecasters warned on Friday that a line of severe storms could bring heavy rain and strong winds to much of the region this weekend.

Officials urged residents in low-lying areas to prepare for possible flooding and to keep emergency supplies on hand.
