City Council Approves Long-Debated Transit Plan

By Jordan Reporter

The council voted on Tuesday to approve a transit plan after months of debate among local officials and residents who packed the chamber.

Supporters said the plan would shorten commutes for thousands of riders, while critics worried about the cost to taxpayers over the coming years.
